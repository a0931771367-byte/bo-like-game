隱私權政策

適用於《口袋冒險團 Pocket Adventurers》iOS 版與網頁版 ｜ 最後更新：2026 年 10 月 28 日

一句話版本

本遊戲是單機遊戲，不會收集、傳送或分享任何個人資料。

我們收集什麼

什麼都不收集。遊戲不要求帳號、不索取姓名、電子郵件、位置、聯絡人或裝置識別碼，也不含任何第三方分析、廣告或追蹤 SDK。

存檔資料

遊戲進度（角色、解鎖內容、設定）只儲存在你自己的裝置上：iOS 版存在 App 的本機沙盒，網頁版存在瀏覽器的本機儲存空間。這些資料不會上傳到任何伺服器。刪除 App 或清除瀏覽器資料即會刪除存檔。

TestFlight 測試版

若你透過 Apple TestFlight 參與測試，Apple 可能依其政策收集當機記錄與使用統計並提供給開發者，相關規範請見 Apple 的 隱私權政策。你在 TestFlight 內送出的回饋由 Apple 轉交，我們只會用它來改善遊戲。

兒童

本遊戲不收集任何人的資料，自然也不收集兒童的資料。

政策變更

若未來新增需要收集資料的功能（例如線上排行榜），我們會先更新本頁並在 App Store 的 App 隱私標籤中揭露。

Privacy Policy

For Pocket Adventurers (iOS and web) · Last updated: October 28, 2026

In one sentence

Pocket Adventurers is an offline, single-player game. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game requires no account and never asks for your name, email, location, contacts, or device identifiers. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Save data

Your progress (characters, unlocks, settings) is stored only on your device: in the app's local sandbox on iOS, or in your browser's local storage on the web. It is never uploaded. Deleting the app or clearing browser data deletes the save.

TestFlight builds

If you test the game through Apple TestFlight, Apple may collect crash logs and usage statistics under its own policies and share them with us. See Apple's Privacy Policy. Feedback you submit through TestFlight is forwarded by Apple and used only to improve the game.

Children

Because the game collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If a future feature requires data collection (for example, online leaderboards), we will update this page first and disclose it in the App Store privacy label.
